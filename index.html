How it works

Click anywhere on your photo.

Drag the box to move it.

Drag the blue corner to resize it. The number automatically gets bigger or smaller with the box.

Double-click a price to edit it.

Select a box and press Delete or Backspace to remove it.

When finished, click Download PNG.
