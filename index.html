How to use

Upload your card photo.

Click anywhere on the photo and a white 100 price box will appear.

Drag the box to move it.

Drag the blue corner to resize it. The number automatically becomes bigger as the box gets bigger.

Double-click the number to change it.

Select a box and press Delete or Backspace to remove it.

Adjust brightness, contrast and saturation on the left.

Download the finished image when you're done.
